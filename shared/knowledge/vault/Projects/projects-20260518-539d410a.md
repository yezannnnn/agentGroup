Aegis-v2: AI Agent命令安全拦截系统。技术栈: NestJS+Vue3+SQLite+Socket.IO+TypeScript。核心流程: PreToolUse Hook → POST /api/v1/rules/evaluate → AST解析(unbash库) → 11套YAML规则匹配 → block/review/allow/warn四种动作。review时WebSocket广播前端弹窗60s超时。关键模块: rules(AST+规则引擎)/approval(审批流)/monitoring(事件持久化)/websocket(实时推送)/storage(SQLite)。前端单页Dashboard含StatsGrid/EventList/AgentGrid/ApprovalModal。支持Claude Code和Hermes两种Hook。规则热加载无需重启。数据库: ~/.aegis/data/aegis.db含events/approvals/sessions三张表。
